Debt Rush is a chaotic 2 co-op action game where you and your partner a shared budget failed in their dream business and have to escape from the debt collectors with whatever they have left.

**Inspiration:**
The game comes from an interest to understand how 2 persons communicate and negotiate. It comes from a desire to understand each other and how the players can handle decisions when they are sharing their budget. It's not only for couples but for friends and strangers to understand how we can build a better world when we communicate. It's also about having fun meanwhile we understand this!

**Gameplay:**
The game occurs in 2 phases: the suburbs as the tutorial area, and the city as the main playground. Each player has their own role and weapon. The first player uses offensive weapons like guns, while the second player focuses more on defensive and supportive items like shields, bombs, and trap mines. Each time a player kills an enemy, they gain money for their shared budget.

**Player's Autonomy:**
Both players can stay together or explore on their own. When they're together, the camera focuses on both players on screen, but when they move far from each other, the camera splits to follow each player, giving them their own autonomy. The Dialogue System was designed and implemented to support this autonomy - players see the same conversation when they're together, or separate ones when they're apart.

**Shared Budget and Secret Missions:**
For each run, players will decide if they have secret individual objectives. Each player selects their mission and finds out at the end of the run whether they completed it. These could be related to exploration, killing more enemies than your partner, or creating competition between you and them. You will also buy weapons and items with the shared budget before each run and this is the time when they have to negotiate the budget and the strategy.
