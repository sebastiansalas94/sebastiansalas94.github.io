**Inspiration:**\
The game comes from an interest to understand how 2 persons communicate and negotiate. It comes from a desire to understand each other and how we handle decisions when we are sharing our budget. It's not only for couples but for friends and strangers to understand how we can build a better world when we communicate. It's also about having fun meanwhile we understand this!
