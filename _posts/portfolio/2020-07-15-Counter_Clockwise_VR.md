Counter Clockwise VR is a replica of a New England House from 1980s to research reminiscence therapy in older adults to understand how a digital space of their childhood affect their mental wellbeing.

**Interactive objects throughout the house:**

A multi-channel TV with working video playback, a radio with a cassette, a coffee machine, inspectable items, and other period-accurate interactions.

**Design and progression system:**

A task and progression system tracking participants' tasks within the environment to guide them through the playrun, with supporting UI (status panels, hints, completion states) indicating to the user when a task has been completed

**Optimizing for standalone hardware:**

The project is built in Unity with the XR Interaction Toolkit and OpenXR, targeting standalone VR headsets such as the Meta Quest 3.
