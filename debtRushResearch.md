For this study we created a multiplayer game for 2 players to simulate a scenario where the users are escaping from the debt collectors (enemies). This game targets young adults and friends or partners, with the objective of exploring how the management of shared financial resources shapes coordination and strategic decision-making between players. By simulating financial interactions within a controlled environment, the game aims to provide insights into how shared resource management and asymmetric roles encourage players to communicate and work
together.
